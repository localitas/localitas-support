# Billing & Plans

## Tunnel Plan

**$20/month** or **$200/year** (save $40)

Includes:
- Unlimited tunnels
- 100 MB/s bandwidth per tunnel
- Custom subdomains
- HTTPS included

## How Billing Works

- Subscriptions are managed through Stripe
- You can subscribe, update payment methods, and view invoices from the **Billing** page in the dashboard
- Cancel anytime; your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period

## Payment Methods

We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe.

## Invoices

All invoices are available in the Billing page. You can download PDF invoices for your records.

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription from the Billing page. After cancellation:
- Your tunnels remain active until the end of your current billing period
- No further charges are made
- You can resubscribe at any time

## Account Deletion

If you delete your account:
- Your account is deactivated immediately
- You have 30 days to recover your account by contacting support
- After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted
